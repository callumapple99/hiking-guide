The Awkward Hiker

Our Key Criteria

So what do we think is valuable information to know before setting off?



There's a wealth of questions you might ask yourself before leaving the house, so we've compiled a list of the most important information that we think you should know before setting off on your next adventure!

Remember that both our criteria and the world are constantly evolving. If you've come here looking for information that we don't have please let us know, we welcome suggestions that improve the quality of our site and makes hiking more accessible to everyone.

Alternatively if you get out and about only to find that one of our reviews is out of date then leave your own to keep our site as accurate as possible and to help the next person looking for information. Remember roads close, bathrooms get renovated and trail conditions can deteriorate, so if you'd want to know about this beforehand then so will someone else - leave a hike review and keep your fellow hikers as up to date as you.

As a last note you might find a lot of questions to ask or get answered below. No one expects you to spend your well earned post-hike euphoria writing a full debrief of your experiences, but if you benefitted from the guidance on this site (or you think someone else can benefit from yours!) then whatever information you can share will be greatly appreciated.

The Journey Before the Journey

What was the starting point of your hike and how did you find getting there? Was the road condition good? Should a nervous driver be aware that the roads were single track, or did you find the directions difficult to follow at any point? Did you take public transport, drive or teleport to your destination?

Any information you can provide will be certain to help the next awkward hiker!

Note: While detailed information is helpful to everyone we ask that you ensure you don't leave any information that could lead back to your home.
If you drove then leave guidance from the closest motorway or major road junction you took, or from the city you started at.

Parking

How easy did you find parking?

Was the carpark large or small? Did you have to pay to park - if so was it by card or cash, was exact change required?

Would you recommend the carpark or would you suggest finding a different site or starting point?

Was the carpark close to the beginning of the trail you took?

When providing a review try to remember how confident a driver you are - if you've been driving for 30 years your experience may differ from a beginner. This experience level logic is one of the key fundamentals that spawned The Awkward Hiker.

Bathrooms and other amenities

Are there bathrooms on the route - condition, paid? Any shops, cafes or pubs? Is there equipment hire for bikes, canoes etc.

How busy is the route?

Some people love a social hike, others prefer their own company. Where there lots of people around? If you had a fall, would you be likely to be found by someone else?

Route Condition and Difficulty

Is the route in good condition? Did you have to pass any objects that others may find difficult, perhaps stiles? Did you require equipment? Overgrown?

Route Ease to Follow and Right of Way

Was the route easy to follow? Did you have to take any diversions? Where you on public land or using farmers gardens?
