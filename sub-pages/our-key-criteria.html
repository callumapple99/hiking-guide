The Awkward Hiker

Our Key Criteria

So what do we think is valuable information to know before setting off?
There's a wealth of questions you might ask yourself before leaving the house, so we've compiled a list of the most important information that we think you should know before setting off for your next adventure!
Remember that both our criteria and the world are constantly evolving. If you've come here looking for information that we don't have please let us know, we welcome suggestions that improve the quality of our site and makes hiking more accessible to everyone. And if you get out and about only to find that one of our reviews is out of date, leave your own to help the next person. Remember roads close, bathrooms get renovated and trail conditions can detiriorate, so if you'd want to know about this beforehand then so will someone else - leave a hike review and keep your fellow hikers as up to date as you.

The Journey Before the Journey

Paragraph about how you got to the starting point - Road Condition, Traffic, Public transport.

Parking

How is the parking - paid, easy to park, public or local.

Bathrooms and other amenities

Are there bathrooms on the route - condition, paid? Any shops, cafes or pubs? Is there equipment hire for bikes, canoes etc.

How busy is the route?

Some people love a social hike, others prefer their own company. Where there lots of people around? If you had a fall, would you be likely to be found by someone else?

Is there phone signal?

Route Condition and Difficulty

Is the route in good condition? Did you have to pass any objects that others may find difficult, perhaps stiles? Did you require equipment? Overgrown?

Route Ease to Follow and Right of Way

Was the route easy to follow? Did you have to take any diversions? Where you on public land or using farmers gardens?
