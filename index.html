About Us

Hiking is one of the most popular and easiest to enter hobbies available.

There's countless sites, apps and pages dedicated to finding the best routes, trails and mountain passes that the country has to offer.

Yet for people like myself the hiking isn't the hard part - it's the logisitcs. Some people are happy to get up, leave the house and figure everything out on route, but not everyone.

Before I set off I want to know

  • What's the journey to the start point going to be like?
    • Is it a single track road?
    • How accessible is the parking?
    • Can I get there by public transport?
  • How busy is the route going to be?
  • Is there going to be phone service?
  • Do I need equipment?
  • Is there a bathroom?
  • How difficult is the hike?

All of these questions can put off first time hikers before they've even left the door.

If you want to find the best hidden locations, which routes are available or which hiking boots are the best then sites like Alltrails, OSMaps and even Tiktok are the best places to look.

But if you have a hike in mind and want the most information you can get before setting off, then this is the site for you.

Take a look at some of our favourite community submission photos from other users of The Awkward Hiker

This page is run and maintained voluntarily. While we'll always try our hardest to give you the best and most accurate information, it's important to remember that everyone's hiking experience is unique.

If you've had a different experience we'd love for you to let us know about it, and if you've recently done a hike and want to help others out you can upload it here to help other budding hikers partake in one of the best activities in the world!
